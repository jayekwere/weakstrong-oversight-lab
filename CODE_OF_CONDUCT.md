# Community code of conduct
Treat participants respectfully. Discuss evidence and ideas without harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or disclosure of private information. Give constructive criticism, accept corrections and credit others' work.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or close discussions to protect the community. Report concerns through a private security report when sensitive information is involved; routine moderation concerns can be raised through a repository issue without naming private individuals. Reports are reviewed proportionately and confidential details are not reposted.
